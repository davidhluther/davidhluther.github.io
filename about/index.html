Now

I'm Senior Director of Enterprise Discoverability and Brand Strategy at Corpay, a payments company on the S&P 500. I report to the CMO and lead content strategy, AI deployment in marketing, brand, and public relations with a team of nine. I joined as a senior manager in February 2025, became director of integrated marketing that August, and was promoted to senior director in July 2026. In that time I've led the national Keep Business Moving rebrand, grown the company's AI search program from a one-person pilot into a funded one, and joined the AI Center of Excellence, where I represent marketing and wrote the company's guidance for using AI at work. The specific results live on my resume rather than a public page, and the method is all in the AEO work.

In April 2026 I founded Pigasus Group, a consultancy that shows small businesses and nonprofits what AI models say about them and helps fix what's wrong. The lab is where I build and test those methods first, usually for an organization near me that needed something and had no budget for it.
