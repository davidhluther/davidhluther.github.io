About

SEO and content strategy director specializing in answer engine optimization for B2B payments. I've spent 14 years turning complex financial and ERP products into content that gets found, and the last two building the systems that decide what "found" means when the finder is an AI.

Now

I'm Director of SEO & Content Strategy at Corpay, an S&P 500 corporate payments company, where I own answer engine optimization as a growth channel across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, and Google AI Overviews. I joined as a senior manager in early 2025 and was promoted to director in 2026, after building the company's AI-visibility measurement program from zero along with the site-readiness infrastructure, analytics forensics, content engine, and reporting pipeline the case studies describe. The specific results live in my resume rather than on a public page. The methodology is all here, and it travels.

Alongside the day job I run Pigasus Group, an independent consultancy and product lab where every system is designed and built solo, from the RAG knowledge base and hybrid retrieval engine to the AI-visibility instrument and the design and evidence gates around them.

Before

As Digital Strategy Senior Manager for NetSuite I led go-to-market web strategy and product narrative for ERP and billing products, built the $2.4M business case that influenced the NetSuite roadmap, and watched competitive win rate climb from 11% to 18% behind the repositioned narrative. Before that I ran SEO-driven content programs across NetSuite's product lines as Senior Content Marketing Manager, tripling web traffic, and built product marketing for the Bronto e-commerce platform as Senior Content Manager, where self-serve trial signups rose 20%.

Earlier, senior SEO and content roles at ROI Revolution, Truist (contract), Zippia, and Eli Global, back to 2011. Before any of that, six years in the U.S. Army National Guard as a Public Affairs Specialist and Cavalry Scout, which is where I learned to write fast under conditions with no patience for filler.
